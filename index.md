I am a PhD Candidate at Princeton in Ecology and Evolution, where I study evolutionary transitions in symbiosis. My work is supported by a [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/about/about-grfp/) and the Robert May Fellowship at Princeton.

I am also a Researcher at [Fairer Fields](https://fairerfields.org/), where I build our in-house mathematical models of ecological interventions.

Previously, I was a Summer Research Fellow at [Rethink Priorities](https://rethinkpriorities.org/) (2026) and a visiting researcher at the [Santa Fe Institute](https://www.santafe.edu/) (2025). I hold a U.S. utility patent for methods to degrade low-density polyethylene and treat landfill leachate.

I earned my Bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics at Columbia, where I was President of the [National Undergraduate Consortium of Science Journalism](https://thenucsj.org/).
